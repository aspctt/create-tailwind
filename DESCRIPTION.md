Create: Tailwind adds compact jetpacks and boosters that run on compressed air. Worn alone, they let you fly the way creative mode does. Worn with an elytra, they act as a booster instead and push your glide forward. So far there is one, the Netherite Jetpack, and it does both.

<p align="center" style="text-align: center;"><img alt="Flying - Up" src="https://i.ibb.co/wZWdT5rY/Create-Tailwind-Flying-Up.png"></p>

### The Netherite Jetpack

Craft it from a netherite backtank, a nether star, two sturdy sheets, two brass sheets and two propellers. Wear it in the chest slot, or in a back slot from [Curios](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios) or [Accessories](https://modrinth.com/mod/accessories), and double-tap jump to fly the way creative mode does. Flight stays at the normal flying speed and draws air from the jetpack, so it cuts out when the tank runs dry. Fall damage still applies, which is worth remembering before that happens.

### Compressed air

The jetpack holds air like Create's backtank and charges the same way. Set it down, turn the shaft input on top and pick it back up with an empty hand. It keeps its air, enchantments and name through placing and breaking, and takes the Capacity enchantment. Create's diving helmet and air-powered tools draw on its air from any of its slots.

### Elytra boost

Wear an elytra as well and the jetpack stops granting flight. Hold forward while gliding and it pushes you the way you are looking, up to a top speed, drawing air as it goes. Either one can take the chest slot while the other goes in a slot from Curios or Accessories, or both can go in slots. The elytra fits a Curios back slot with [Elytra Slot](https://modrinth.com/mod/elytra-slot), and Create: Tailwind then adds a second back slot so both fit. With Accessories it goes in the cape slot through [Accessories Compat: Vanilla](https://modrinth.com/mod/accessories-compat-vanilla). The boost works with or without Do Another Barrel Roll, and its acceleration and top speed default to Do a Barrel Roll's thrusting.

### Exhaust

Each nozzle blows a glowing jet of white air that grows with speed, along with amber sparks and a short smoke trail. The nozzle openings glow white hot while the jetpack runs, then cool through ember colours back to black over about eight seconds. The engine is a rush of air with Create's cogwheel rumble and a low hum behind it, and the tank vents a steam hiss on takeoff and every few seconds after.

### Configuration

Each player can turn off the flame and glow, the sparks, the smoke and the engine sound. They can also thin out the trail and set the engine volume. The server sets the air cost and the boost's acceleration and top speed, and decides whether fall damage applies and whether flying removes Invisibility. The config screen needs [YACL](https://modrinth.com/mod/yacl); without it the config files can be edited by hand.

### Requirements

Create: Tailwind needs Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.250 or newer and [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) 6.0.10 or newer in the 6.0 line, on both the client and the server. Optional: Curios or Accessories for back slots, Elytra Slot or Accessories Compat: Vanilla to wear an elytra in a slot and YACL for the config screen.
